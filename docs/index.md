# 4D-Bio

4D-Bio is a community-maintained map of the skills needed to practice AI-assissted bioinformatics responsibly and effectively. It organizes key bioinformatics skills, derived from the [ISCB Competency Framework v3.0](https://competency.ebi.ac.uk/framework/iscb/3.0) and selected publications, around four AI-fluency capabilities from
[Anthropic's 4D framework for AI fluency](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/334975cdec18f744b4fa511dc8518bd8d119d29d.pdf?ref=hackshackers.com):
**Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence**.  

[Read the map](overview.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

---

## The four capabilities

| Capability | Anthropic's definition | Meaning in bioinformatics |
|---|---|---|
| **[Delegation](delegation.md)** | Setting goals and deciding whether, when, and how to engage with AI | Breaking complex problems into small independent tasks and determining the best bioinformatics tools to use for each. |
| **[Description](description.md)** | Effectively describing goals to prompt useful AI behaviors and outputs. | communicating goals, context, and biological assumptions to AI agents in the form of technical specifications. |
| **[Discernment](discernment.md)** | Accurately assessing the usefulness of AI outputs and behaviors | Using deep biological intuition to identify mistakes in AI-assisted analyses. |
| **[Diligence](diligence.md)** | Taking responsibility for what we do with AI and how we use it. | Assuming accountability for the impact of agentic AI solutions on the global life sciences community. |

## Who this is for

- **Practicing bioinformaticians**: self-assess where your AI skills are strong or thin.
- **Educators and curriculum designers**: decide what to teach and how to train the next generation.
- **Team leads and hiring managers**: write job descriptions and AI-use guidelines grounded in a shared vocabulary.

## How to use it

1. Read the **[Overview](overview.md)** to see how the model is built and how its source frameworks fit together.
2. Pick a capability and read its **practice areas**. 
3. Think about how each practice area connects to your daily activites and see what skills could help you.

## Contributing

4D-Bio is an early draft and a call to the bioinformatics community. As domain experts, it is on us to decide how generative AI enters our daily work and our training programs.

See the **[How to contribute](contributing.md)** page for every way to help, from fixing a typo to proposing new additions. Use the pencil icon at the top-right of any page to propose changes.
