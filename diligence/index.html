    Diligence¶

    Diligence is "taking responsibility for what we do with AI and how we use it" (Anthropic's 4D Framework for AI fluency). In bioinformatics, it means assuming accountability for the impact of agentic AI solutions. This is not only about adhering to international standards for ethical and safe AI use, but also maintaining the cognitive abilities to remain critical of AI-generated outputs and complete tasks manually when AI agents fail.

    Category Competency Source
    Professional Conduct J3: Comply with professional, ethical, legal, and social standards. ISCB
    AI regulatory alignment Monitor rapidly shifting regional and global AI legal frameworks to dynamically align pipeline deployments with local jurisdiction mandates. 4D-Bio
    Ethical evaluation Identify algorithmic bias and ensure that AI-driven decisions are calibrated, safe, and legally compliant. 4D-Bio
      Formulate institutional/team risk guardrails to prevent algorithmic discrimination, dual-use biological research hazards, or intellectual property infringements. 4D-Bio

    3. Countering mental dependency while preserving technical mastery¶

    Category Competency Source
    Iterative skill development Counter "cognitive offloading" (the habit of delegating reasoning to tools) by intentionally practicing the "cognitive musculature" of manual coding and statistical reasoning. ACTIVE
    Professional Conduct M3: Engage in continuing professional development. ISCB

    5. Guiding institutional strategy and cross-sector diplomacy¶

    Category Competency Source
    Visionary stewardship Design long-term technical roadmaps, governance models, and sustainability plans to secure the permanent operational longevity of bioinformatics initiatives. 4D-Bio
    Scientific diplomacy Navigate complex negotiations and communicate impact across academia, industry, and government sectors to align competing interests and influence decision makers. 4D-Bio

    6. Elevating workforce capacity and educational legacy¶

    Category Competency Source
    Generational mentorship Develop early-career talent by actively mentoring them to leverage generative AI systems appropriately, critically, and responsibly. 4D-Bio
    Pedagogical dissemination Design open-access learning resources that leverage cloud and AI technologies to democratize access to scientific discoveries and dismantle structural educational inequities. 4D-Bio
